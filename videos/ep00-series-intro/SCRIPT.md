## Recording tips
- Record on your phone's voice memo app in a quiet room with soft furnishings (a closet works well), holding the phone about 20 cm from your mouth.
- Record one take of the whole script and leave 1 s of silence at the start. Small slips are fine: I'll cut around them.
- Send the file as m4a, mp3 or wav.
